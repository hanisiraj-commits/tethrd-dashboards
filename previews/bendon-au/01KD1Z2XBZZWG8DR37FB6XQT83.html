Hi , 2025 was a big year.

And in a world that’s felt a little too heavy, we’ve been reminded how much the small things matter. Feeling supported, understood, and good in your own skin. That’s what guided us this year, alongside you.

We spent time listening. Really listening.
We took on your feedback, learned from it, and made some big calls, including saying goodbye to two of our brands. While that chapter closed, the styles you’ve always loved didn’t disappear. They live on, because the things that work, are worth holding onto.

We introduced Bra Better. From the Post Divorce Bra to the Strap Them In Bra and the Boob Job in a Bra, we leaned into real life, the moments you’ve shared with us, and matched them with beautiful design.

We refreshed our stores across Australia and New Zealand, creating spaces that feel more like us. We also quietly opened our doors a little wider, launching online across Europe, the UK, Singapore, and Hong Kong, so wherever life takes you, we can come along too.

Behind the scenes, we’ve kept doing what we love, dreaming up new collections and ideas, and finishing a campaign we’re excited to share with you early in 2026. None of this happens without our incredible team, the people in our stores and offices who care deeply and listen closely, to make sure you feel supported, not sold to.

But the best part of 2025 has been you.
Your stories. Your honesty. Your trust.

Thank you for letting us be part of your moments, big, small, and everything in between. We can’t wait to see you in 2026.

 
Anna Johnson
CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER
Bendon. Bra Better.
